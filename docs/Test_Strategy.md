Test Strategy
Overview

This document describes the quality strategy used for the IoT Hydration Monitoring Platform.

The objective of this strategy is to provide confidence that measurements generated by connected devices are processed correctly, securely and reliably while protecting user privacy and ensuring clinically meaningful outputs.

Testing priorities were determined using a risk-based approach focused on business impact, patient trust, device reliability and data integrity.

Quality Goals

The testing approach aims to validate:

Accurate measurement processing
Reliable hydration analysis
Secure user identification and data access
Robust handling of connectivity issues
Safe firmware management
Protection against data corruption and duplication
System resilience under backend failures

Assumptions

The challenge specification leaves several implementation areas open to interpretation.

The following assumptions were made:

Duplicate detection is based on device and session identifiers.
Firmware updates can fail due to version incompatibility.
Hydration scores must remain within expected bounds.
Backend outages require retry mechanisms.
Anonymous users are valid platform users.
Devices may reconnect and upload buffered measurements.
Conclusion

This strategy prioritises quality efforts according to business risk rather than raw endpoint coverage.
